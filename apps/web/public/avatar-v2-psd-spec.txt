STREAM BRO PSD AVATAR V2 SPEC

DOCUMENT
- One half-body avatar on a transparent canvas.
- Square canvas is recommended. 2048 x 2048 is recommended for source art.
- RGB color mode, 8 bits per channel.
- Use normal raster layers for the final file.
- Keep every part in its final aligned position.
- Groups are allowed. Leaf layer names must be unique.
- Layer names are case-insensitive. Spaces and underscores become hyphens.
- Hide alternate mouths and closed eyes when saving. Stream Bro controls visibility.

MOUTH SHAPES
mouth-idle = relaxed closed mouth
mouth-a = wide vertical opening
mouth-i = wide horizontal smile shape
mouth-u = small rounded and pushed shape
mouth-e = medium horizontal opening
mouth-o = large rounded opening

ART RULES
- Draw complete art under moving parts so no holes appear during motion.
- Eye whites must include the open eye outline.
- Eye balls must include iris, pupil, and highlight.
- Closed-eye layers replace eye-white and eye-ball layers during a blink.
- Hair-back goes behind the body and head. Hair-front goes above the face.
- Do not use duplicate required names.
- PSD/PSB large-document files are not supported. Use PSD.
